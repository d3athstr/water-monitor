PARTS (8)

  U2     ADS1115 16-bit I2C ADC @ 0x48                     on the 5 V rail
  P1     Pressure transducer, 0-100 psi ratiometric        inlet (P1)
  F1     Flow meter, pulse output
  T1     DS18B20 temperature sensor, 1-Wire
  L1,L2  Leak sensors x2
  BZ1    Buzzer
  (P2)   Outlet pressure transducer - PHASE 2, not fitted

NETS (9)

  NET FLOW_PULSE  U1.GPIO4    F1.pulse

  NET LEAK1       U1.GPIO6    L1
  NET LEAK2       U1.GPIO7    L2
  NET BUZZER      U1.GPIO10   BZ1
  NET 5V/GND      U2.VDD      P1.VCC   (common ground with U1)

WHY THE ADS1115 AND NOT THE ESP32'S OWN ADC
  P1 is ratiometric, 0.5 V at 0 psi and 4.5 V at 100 psi, referenced to a 5 V
  supply. The ADS1115 sits on that same 5 V rail, so it reads the transducer
  DIRECTLY - no divider, and no divider tolerance stack-up in the reading.
  psi = (V - 0.5) * 25

  Contrast the pool-pressure-sensor project, which puts a 10k/22k divider in
  front of a bare ESP32 ADC pin. Same class of sensor, different trade: fewer
  parts there, better accuracy and no over-voltage risk here.

PHASE 2 - differential pressure
  P2 on U2.A1, plus a template sensor for P1 - P2. Both are written and
  commented out in esphome/packages/pressure.yaml. Filter/softener dP is the
  reason the ADC has spare channels.

CONDUCTIVITY - scoped, not built
  Commented out in esphome/packages/conductivity.yaml. It needs a sample valve
  on a spare GPIO via a relay (GPIO11 was the placeholder), and an EC probe at
  I2C 0x64. Do not treat it as present.

SITE ADDRESSING - keep it out of this repo
  THIS REPO IS PUBLIC. The ESPHome manual_ip block reads static_ip / gateway /
  dns1 from !secret, with placeholders in esphome/secrets.yaml.example and the
  real secrets.yaml gitignored. The docs say "IoT VLAN" rather than naming
  addresses. Keep it that way - the history was rebuilt once already to get the
  real addresses out of the blobs before the first push.
